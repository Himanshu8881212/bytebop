Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

ByteBop is an individual creator publishing AI explainer videos. ByteBop Publisher is a desktop tool used only by the ByteBop creator.

What data the tool uses

When the creator signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and the account's basic profile (open ID and display name) through TikTok Login Kit. It uses them only to confirm the account and to upload and post the creator's own videos through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a file readable by that user. Nothing is stored on any server we run.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. We do not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users.

Deletion

The creator can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps) and by deleting the local token file.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Himanshu8881212/bytebop.
